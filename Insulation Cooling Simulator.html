Insulation cooling simulator

Warm water in a wrapped container loses heat through the insulation (Fourier’s law) and cools following Newton’s law of cooling. Change any value on the left and the graphs update instantly. Results are calculated and simulated, not measured.

Inputs (sample data loaded)

Materials: conductivity K (W/m·K)

Safe range: keep the starting temperature below 90 °C. K values are approximate; check your textbook.

How to read the graphs: solid lines and filled bars show your current values. Dashed lines and outlined bars show the original sample data, so you can see how your changes compare. Move the mouse (or finger) over a graph to read exact values.
